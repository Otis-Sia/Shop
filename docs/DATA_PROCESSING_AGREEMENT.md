## TERMS OF AGREEMENT

### 1. Definitions
Unless otherwise defined, terms such as *"Personal Data"*, *"Data Subject"*, *"Processing"*, *"Sub-processor"*, and *"Data Breach"* have the meanings ascribed under the Kenyan Data Protection Act, 2019 and GDPR.

### 2. Subject Matter and Duration
- **2.1 Scope:** The Processor shall process Personal Data on behalf of the Controller solely to provide contracted services (including payment gateway processing, cloud database hosting, logistics delivery, analytics, or transactional notifications).
- **2.2 Duration:** Processing shall continue for the term of the underlying service agreement and any subsequent period required by legal, regulatory, or tax retention obligations.

### 3. Types of Personal Data & Data Subjects
- **3.1 Data Types:** Identity data (name, user ID), contact data (email, phone number, shipping address), transaction data (order history, invoices), financial data (transaction identifiers, payment status), and technical/device data (IP addresses, user agents).
- **3.2 Data Subjects:** Customers and registered users of Cepine Juj4, site visitors, and personnel.

### 4. Nature and Purpose of Processing
The Processor shall process Personal Data exclusively to:
1. Provide the contracted cloud, payment, or logistics services;
2. Facilitate transaction execution and order fulfilment;
3. Maintain data storage, security, and fraud prevention;
4. Deliver customer support and analytics reporting.

### 5. Obligations of the Controller
The Controller warrants that:
1. It maintains a lawful basis for processing under the Kenyan DPA 2019;
2. It provides clear and documented instructions to the Processor;
3. It respects and facilitates the statutory rights of Data Subjects.

### 6. Obligations of the Processor
The Processor shall:
- **6.1 Documented Instructions:** Process Personal Data solely in accordance with documented instructions from the Controller.
- **6.2 Confidentiality:** Ensure all personnel authorized to process data are bound by formal confidentiality obligations.
- **6.3 Security Measures:** Implement technical and organizational measures (encryption in transit TLS 1.2+, AES-256 at rest, role-based access control, regular security audits).
- **6.4 Data Subject Requests:** Assist the Controller in fulfilling Data Subject requests (access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability) within statutory timeframes.
- **6.5 Breach Notification:** Notify the Controller without undue delay (**and in any event within 48 hours**) upon becoming aware of any actual or suspected Personal Data Breach.
- **6.6 Return or Deletion:** Securely delete or return all Personal Data upon termination of services, certifying completion in writing.
- **6.7 Regulatory Cooperation:** Cooperate with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) in Kenya and relevant supervisory authorities.

### 7. Sub-processors
- **7.1 Authorized Sub-processors:** The Controller authorizes the Processor to engage sub-processors necessary for service delivery (see Annex A).
- **7.2 Prior Notification:** The Processor must notify the Controller before appointing new sub-processors, granting the Controller the right to object on reasonable data protection grounds.
- **7.3 Flow-down Terms:** The Processor shall impose data protection terms on all sub-processors no less protective than those in this Agreement.

### 8. International Data Transfers
The Processor shall not transfer Personal Data outside Kenya or the EEA without prior written authorization and implementation of approved transfer mechanisms (such as ODPC-approved Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions).

### 9. Liability and Indemnity
Each party is liable for damages resulting from its breach of this Agreement or data protection laws. The Processor shall indemnify the Controller against fines, penalties, or claims arising from Processor breach or unauthorized actions.

### 10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **Republic of Kenya**. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of Kenya.

- **Access Control:** Multi-factor authentication (MFA) and least-privilege role-based access control (RBAC).
- **Vulnerability Scanning:** Automated dependency scanning and continuous monitoring.
- **Incident Response:** Documented escalation protocol for security incidents with 48-hour breach notification guarantee.
